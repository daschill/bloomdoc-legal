PLANT CARE THAT REMEMBERS

Know your plants.
Notice what changes.

BloomDoc turns a photo into a careful starting point, then keeps the dates, observations, reminders, and follow-ups that help you learn what each plant needs.

01

Start with one plant

Photograph a single plant, review the identification confidence, and retake unclear or crowded photos before saving anything.

02

Care, then observe

Keep a dated garden, set optional local reminders, and record what you actually did—not what a calendar assumes.

03

Check safety cautiously

Review separate people, cat, and dog references. Unknown or conflicting evidence stays unknown; BloomDoc never turns appearance into a safety claim.

A CAREFUL SECOND OPINION

Useful guidance, honest limits.

Plant identification and toxicity information can be wrong. Never eat a plant based on an app. If exposure may have occurred, contact poison control, a veterinarian, or emergency services instead of waiting for BloomDoc.
