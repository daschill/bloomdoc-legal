Common tasks

Restore a purchase

Open the paywall or Profile and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not directly by BloomDoc.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BloomDoc
  • Android: Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Delete your account and data

In the app: open Profile (person icon) → scroll to Danger zone → Delete account. This removes your plants, scans, care schedules, chat history, and the account. If you cannot open the app, email us and we will help.

Notifications

Watering reminders are scheduled on your phone. Enable them in Profile and in iOS Settings → BloomDoc → Notifications. We do not send marketing push notifications.

Camera or library access

Scanning needs the camera. You can also pick an existing photo. If you denied access, enable Camera / Photos for BloomDoc in system settings.
