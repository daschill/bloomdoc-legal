Support

BloomDoc is made by Schill Labs. We are happy to help with scans, reminders, subscriptions, and your account. Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are linked at the bottom of this page.

Contact

Email mikeschillereff@hotmail.com and include:

  • Your device and iOS / Android version
  • What you expected to happen
  • Whether you are on the free plan or Premium

Common tasks

Restore a purchase

Open the paywall or Profile and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not directly by BloomDoc.

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BloomDoc
  • Android: Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

Delete your account and data

Cloud account deletion and Guest User recovery-code submission are currently unavailable while a photo-cleanup issue is being resolved. Visit the account-deletion page for current service status and support options. You can email a request for help now, but that does not confirm completed deletion. Do not send a password, sign-in token, deletion receipt, recovery code or private plant photos.

A pending request or network timeout is not confirmation that deletion finished. Keep the saved receipt and check its status until cloud and phone cleanup are confirmed. Deleting an account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

A copy of your data

Email mikeschillereff@hotmail.com and ask for access or an export. We must verify account ownership before sharing data. Do not send passwords, tokens or recovery codes. We cannot identify a Guest User from a device name or plant description.

Notifications

Care, plant-project, and room-checkup reminders are scheduled on your phone. Enable the reminder families you want in Profile and allow notifications in iOS or Android system settings. We do not send marketing push notifications.

Camera or library access

Scanning needs the camera. You can also pick an existing photo. If you denied access, enable Camera / Photos for BloomDoc in system settings.
