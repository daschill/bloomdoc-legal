BloomDoc

Delete an account

You can request removal of your BloomDoc account, plants, scans, care schedules, chat history and stored photos. Removing the app from your phone is not an account-deletion request.

Current service status

Cloud account deletion and Guest User recovery-code submission are not currently available. We are resolving a photo-cleanup issue. We cannot confirm completed deletion while that issue remains unresolved.

You can contact support now to request help. A support request is not confirmation of completed deletion, and we cannot promise a completion date yet.

Request help with a saved account

If you saved your account with email or Sign in with Apple, email BloomDoc support with an account-deletion request. Use the address associated with your BloomDoc account if you can. We must verify ownership before acting on an account; an email address alone is not proof.

Do not include your password, sign-in token, deletion receipt, recovery code or private plant photos.

Guest Users and recovery codes

If you still have the original installation, keep it while contacting support. If you saved a recovery code, keep it private for the future recovery service. This page does not collect codes. Do not email a recovery code to anyone: it can authorize permanent deletion.

A Guest User is tied to its original app account, not an email or display name. If the installation and recovery code are both gone, support cannot safely identify it from a device name or a plant description and will not guess which account to remove.

Keep any pending receipt

If your app has already accepted a deletion request, keep its status receipt. An accepted or pending request, an empty screen, and a network timeout are not completed deletion. Do not assume that photos were removed because the app stopped showing them.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting a BloomDoc account or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. See Support for subscription-management instructions.
