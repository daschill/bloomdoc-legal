Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026 · Operator: Schill Labs (Mike Schillereff)

This policy describes how BloomDoc (“the app”) handles information when you identify plants, save a collection, subscribe, or contact us. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Plant photos you choose to scan or save. Photos are uploaded so the app can identify species, estimate health, and keep a record in My Plants.
  • Account data. The app creates an anonymous account so your plants and scans can be stored. If you add an email and password, we store that email so you can sign in on another device.
  • Scan results, care plans, and chat with Leafy. These are stored with your account so you can reopen them later.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. We receive entitlement status (for example, whether Premium is active), not your full payment card number.
  • Usage counts. Daily and monthly free-tier scan and chat counts are checked on our backend so limits can be enforced.
  • Product interaction events. Named events such as scan started, plant saved, paywall shown, and subscribe (no advertising identifier). Used to operate and improve the app, not to track you across other companies’ apps.

How we use plant photos and AI

When you scan a plant, the photo is sent to our Supabase backend. A server-side edge function (ai-proxy) forwards the image and a prompt to OpenAI so we can return a species guess, health notes, and a care plan. Photos are used only to provide those features. We do not use your plant photos to train a public advertising profile.

Processors

  • Supabase — authentication, Postgres database, file storage for plant photos, and the edge function that calls the AI provider.
  • OpenAI — vision and text analysis requested by the edge function. The OpenAI API key stays on the server; it is not embedded in the app.
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and restore-purchases for App Store / Google Play products.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — billing for auto-renewable subscriptions.

Anonymous accounts

If you skip email sign-in, BloomDoc still creates an anonymous account so scans can be saved on that device. Anonymous data is tied to that install. Adding an email lets you keep plants if you change phones.

Notifications

Care reminders (watering and fertilizing) are scheduled as local notifications on your device. We do not send server push notifications for care tasks. You can disable reminders in Profile or in system settings.

Location

BloomDoc does not request or read your location.

Data retention

Account data, plant photos, scans, care schedules, and chat history are kept while your account exists so the app can show your collection. If you delete your account in the app, we delete associated rows and stored photos we control. Billing records may be retained by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat as required for tax and fraud prevention. Backups may persist for a short period before they rotate.

Account deletion

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your plants, scans, care schedules, chat history, and the account we created. You can also email mikeschillereff@hotmail.com and we will help.

Children

BloomDoc is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.
